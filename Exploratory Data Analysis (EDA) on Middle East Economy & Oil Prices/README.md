# 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Middle East Economy & Oil Prices Dataset

This project is part of my **Excelerate Internship Program**, where I performed **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a **Middle East Economy & Oil Prices Dataset** to uncover key economic insights and understand the impact of oil price fluctuations on regional economies.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
- 📂 **Dataset**: Middle East Economy & Oil Prices (1990–2024)  
- 🎯 **Objective**: Perform in-depth data exploration, visualization, and analysis to identify trends, relationships, and economic patterns influenced by oil prices.  
- 🧠 **Goal**: Understand GDP growth, inflation, unemployment, exports/imports, and oil price dynamics across major Middle Eastern countries.

---

## Project Tasks

- Conducted **missing values and data quality analysis**  
- Created **trend charts** for GDP growth, inflation, unemployment, and Brent oil prices  
- Visualized **country-wise economic indicators** for major Middle East economies  
- Explored **correlations** between oil prices and economic metrics  
- Identified key patterns, outliers, and seasonal trends  

---

## ⚙️ Tools & Libraries Used
- **Programming Language:** Python  
- **Libraries:**
  - `pandas` → Data manipulation and preprocessing  
  - `numpy` → Numerical operations  
  - `matplotlib` & `seaborn` → Data visualization  
  - `plotly` → Optional interactive visualizations  

---

## 🔍 EDA Process

### Step 1: Import Libraries and Dataset
- Loaded the dataset using **pandas**  
- Previewed initial rows using `df.head()`  
- Checked dataset structure with `df.info()` and summary statistics with `df.describe()`  

### Step 2: Data Cleaning
- Handled missing values using `mean`, `mode`, or interpolation  
- Removed duplicate or inconsistent entries  
- Ensured proper data types for all columns  

### Step 3: Data Visualization
- Plotted **line charts** for GDP growth, inflation, unemployment, and Brent oil prices  
- Created **scatterplots and regression plots** to explore relationships between oil prices and economic indicators  
- Generated **correlation heatmaps** to identify significant associations  

### Step 4: Country-Level Analysis
- Compared **economic trends across major Middle East countries**  
- Analyzed **exports, imports, and FDI inflows** relative to oil prices  

### Step 5: Summary of Insights
- Determined the impact of oil price fluctuations on GDP growth, inflation, and unemployment  
- Highlighted trends in economic diversification across countries  
- Identified years with extreme economic events linked to oil price shocks  

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- Oil prices have a **direct impact on GDP growth** for oil-dependent economies  
- Inflation and unemployment trends often **reflect global oil price shocks**  
- Diversified economies, like UAE and Saudi Arabia, **show resilience** to oil price fluctuations  
- Exports and FDI inflows are significantly influenced by **regional economic stability**
